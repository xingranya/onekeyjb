runtime: adopt the Shark 2.5 runners (2.22.0)

Shark 2.5 moved its exploit binaries from assets to jniLibs and shipped new
builds. Compare against what we ship:

- libk61821.so  451,536 -> 467,120 B  (sha 3f6d407d...)
- libk61269.so  449,544 -> 478,880 B  (sha d51f65f6...)

Both of our kernels stay on the plain runner: neither 6.18 release
(gf957accaf563, g7fcbffcad3e1) appears in isPlantCore61821, so the p/x
variants do not apply.

What changed on our side:
- replace both runner binaries; the APK now carries the new sizes
- 6.18 gained FAIL=plantresultmodule and 6.12 gained contract/mem/probe/
  profile/tracefs/uname; match FAIL= and give each kind an actionable hint
  instead of dumping the raw line
- add four kernel releases to DeviceProfiles: PYB110 284297031c53 and
  3977fbeb3462, PMS110 5fd39602326e and 586bfab1b9c5
- document the protocol: PYB110_GATE/PYB110_CHASE are host-side extensions the
  runner never reads, PYB110_CARRIER is runner-internal

Unchanged on purpose: the assets staging path, the sh -c "env ... run" launch
form, and the human-readable log markers ([n/4], MODULE_LOADED, DONE,
PLANT_CRED_OK) which the new builds still emit alongside EV|v=1|phase=.
